This law is a weaker form of the [central groupoid law 168](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?168).

The finite spectrum is exactly `{1}∪[4,+∞)`. Explicit four-point and five-point
cores with indexed pairs give models of every even and odd order at least four.
This is formalized as `Spectrum.spectrum_1480` in `Spectrum/Equation1480.lean`;
see [the proof](https://github.com/Timeroot/equational_theories/blob/main/docs/1480_finite_spectrum_theorem.md).

This law cannot hold in a (non-trivial) commutative or associative magma or quasigroup.
