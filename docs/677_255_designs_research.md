# Why the current E677 constructions cannot refute E255

**Follow-up:** the [new strong-block construction](strong_design_cofiniteness_20260928.md)
allows nonidempotent group fillings and proves cofiniteness on paper. Its
models still satisfy E255: every one-variable computation stays inside its
group, whose affine or idempotent operation already satisfies E255.

28 September 2026. **None of the idempotent finite-field seeds, the quartic
companion constructions, or the standard Wilson/PBD gluings can produce a
counterexample to E677 implying E255.** Every magma produced by those
constructions is idempotent, and E255 holds in every idempotent magma.

There is also a stronger algebraic restriction: **every scalar-affine E677
operation over any commutative ring satisfies E255**, including nonidempotent
operations and nonzero constant terms. This has been proved in Lean without
finiteness or field assumptions. It covers all the saved finite-field models,
not just the idempotent seeds.

The general finite implication E677 to E255 remains open. These results rule
out construction methods; they do not settle that implication for arbitrary
finite magmas.

## Idempotence and the design construction

The target equation is the one-variable law

    E255: x = ((x*x)*x)*x.

If x*x=x, each of its three products reduces to x. Thus any counterexample
to E255 must have a nonidempotent element. The E677 hypothesis is irrelevant
to this elementary observation.

The quartic construction in `Spectrum/QuarticSeeds.lean` has the form

    x*y = x + T(y-x),

where T is an explicitly specified companion operator on R⁴. In particular
x*x=x over every commutative ring R, independently of the companion
polynomial. The E677 polynomial is

    t⁴-t³+t²-t+1.

Its companion operator gives E677, while idempotence independently gives
E255. Changing the base ring, allowing zero divisors, or taking larger
fourth-power orders does not change this conclusion.

For a pairwise balanced design, put an idempotent E677 operation on each
block. Define x*y using the unique block through distinct x,y, and set
x*x=x. Each two-variable instance of E677 is evaluated within its block,
so the construction preserves E677. For E255 an even simpler argument
applies: there is only one input point, and every intermediate product is
x*x=x. No operation between distinct points is used. Hence E255 holds
regardless of the chosen blocks or their individual idempotent operations.
Wilson's theorem supplies the designs, but does not change this diagonal.

This is a statement about arbitrary idempotent block magmas, not merely
linear or finite-field blocks. PBD gluing therefore cannot escape the
restriction by choosing more exotic idempotent seeds.

## The scalar-affine calculation

Let R be any commutative ring, and let

    x*y = a*x+b*y+c.

Expanding E677, which is

    x = y*(x*((y*x)*y)),

gives three coefficient conditions. Put

    P = a*b³+a*b-1,
    Q = a²*b²+a+b³,
    R0 = a*b²+b²+b+1,
    S = a²+a+1.

Then E677 is equivalent to

    P=0, Q=0, c*R0=0.

Necessity follows by evaluating at (x,y)=(0,0),(1,0),(0,1); sufficiency
follows by expanding the operation. This reasoning is valid with zero
divisors and in infinite rings.

E255 expands to the two conditions

    (a+b-1)*S=0,       c*S=0.

Here are explicit polynomial identities proving both consequences:

    (a+b-1)*S
      = (-a³*b-a²-a*b²-b+1)*P
        + a*(a*b²+a+b-1)*Q,

    S = (-a²*b-a*b-a-2)*P
        + (a*b²+a+b²+b)*Q
        + (-b³+b-1)*R0.

The first identity proves the linear coefficient condition. Multiply the
second by c and use c*R0=0 to prove the constant condition. There is no
division, root classification, size bound, or cancellation assumption.

For an idempotent field seed, a+b=1 and c=0. Substituting a=1-b into P,Q
reduces them to minus and plus the quartic

    b⁴-b³+b²-b+1.

This recovers precisely the companion polynomial used by the existing
construction, and explains why its field realizations are idempotent.

The same polynomial argument also applies to affine operations on an
abelian group whose two coefficient endomorphisms commute: interpret the
polynomials in the commutative ring generated by those endomorphisms.
Subtracting the E677 evaluations at (x,0), (0,y), and (0,0) gives P=Q=0 as
endomorphisms and R0(c)=0. The two displayed identities then give E255.
This module-level extension is a paper consequence, not a separate Lean
declaration in this pass. The formal scalar-affine theorem below concerns
a ring as its carrier.

## Independent checks of the original witnesses

The checker reconstructs the original finite fields and operation tables
from `data/spectrum/open_survey_20260927.json`, and evaluates the original
E677 and E255 expressions directly. It checks all 18 saved general field
witnesses, including their 10 nonidempotent choices, as well as the 9
separately recorded idempotent witnesses. Every one satisfies E255.
The latter list can select a different operation at an already represented
order; the two lists are not claimed to be disjoint.

The checker independently expands both polynomial identities with SymPy.
Its record is
[`data/spectrum/677_255_designs_check.json`](../data/spectrum/677_255_designs_check.json).
These computational checks corroborate the general proof; the Lean theorem
does not depend on the survey data or on SymPy.

## Lean declarations and scope

The standalone module is
[`Spectrum/Equation677/ConstructionLimits.lean`](../equational_theories/Spectrum/Equation677/ConstructionLimits.lean).
Its principal declarations, in namespace `Spectrum.E677.ConstructionLimits`,
are:

* `equation255_of_idempotent`: every idempotent magma satisfies E255;
* `quartic_equation255`: every operation of the existing companion form
  satisfies E255;
* `affine_equation255`: a scalar-affine E677 operation over any commutative
  ring satisfies E255;
* `coefficient_identity` and `constant_identity`: the two displayed
  polynomial identities.

All three main theorems carry `spectrum_assert ... complete`. Their axiom
guards allow, respectively, no axioms, `propext`, and `propext` together with
`Quot.sound`. There is no `sorry`, `native_decide`, or solver certificate.

```sh
lake build equational_theories.Spectrum.Equation677.ConstructionLimits
python3 scripts/spectrum_677_255_designs_check.py
```

E255 is an equation, so products, submagmas, and homomorphic images of these
models also satisfy it. Ordinary idempotent PBD gluing and these usual
closures cannot furnish the desired counterexample. A potentially successful
new construction must leave this class, for example by producing a
nonidempotent operation outside the commuting-affine setting. No claim is
made here that arbitrary isotopies, arbitrary noncommuting affine models,
or arbitrary finite E677 magmas satisfy E255.

## Left-division reformulation: E9321 → E203 (23 October 2026)

There is an exact bijection between finite E677 models with operation `*`
and finite E9321 models with operation `⋆`, using actual left division:

```
x ⋆ y = x \ y = y * ((x*y)*x)
x * y = (y⋆x) ⋆ (y⋆(y⋆x)).
E9321: x = y ⋆ ((x⋆y) ⋆ (x⋆(x⋆y))).
```

In particular, the E9321 rows are permutations. Its different elements
also index different rows. Right bijectivity is not guaranteed: the finite
25- and 49-element E677 counterexamples to right cancellation transfer to
E9321 under left division. Idempotence is preserved by the correspondence,
so restricting a counterexample search to idempotent models still cannot help.
Finite affine models remain affine after taking left division; thus the
existing affine obstruction carries over as well.

The target becomes a shorter-looking unary condition in these coordinates:

```
E677 → E255 for all finite models
  iff
E9321 → E203 for all finite models,

E203: x = (x ⋆ (x⋆x)) ⋆ x.
```

The key calculation, valid whenever the actual left division exists, is

```
(x*x)*x = x ⋆ (x⋆x).
```

Also `y*x=x` iff `y⋆x=x`: a permutation and its inverse fix the same points.
Consequently, with `r(x)=x⋆(x⋆x)`, a finite E9321 model satisfies E203 at
`x` iff there exists `y` with `y⋆x=x`; any such `y` must equal `r(x)`.
The fixed-point sets of different row permutations are therefore disjoint.
The remaining question is whether they cover the carrier. Equivalently,
is `sum_y |Fix(L_y)|` always the carrier size? It is at most that size by
uniqueness. Bijective columns suffice, but are not necessary.

These statements are formalized in
[`Equation677Parastrophe.lean`](../equational_theories/Parastrophes/Equation677Parastrophe.lean),
including `left_term`, `rows_injective`, `cubic_transfer`,
`equation255_at_iff203`, `fixer_unique`, `equation203_at_iff_fixer`, and
`finite_question_equiv`. The last theorem proves equivalence of the two
questions on **each fixed finite carrier**. It does not prove either open
implication.

### E255 by itself

E255 does not force either left or right bijectivity: every idempotent magma
satisfies it, including the two projection operations. Its actual division
images therefore refer to the subfamilies where the chosen division exists.

For right division, E255 is preserved in both directions, without finiteness.
Writing `R_x(t)=t*x`, it says `R_x^3(x)=x`, which is unchanged on replacing
`R_x` by its inverse. Thus the right image is precisely the right quasigroups
satisfying E255.

For left division, a model satisfies the transferred E255 condition at `x`
iff there are `a,b` with

```
x⋆a=x,  a⋆b=x,  b⋆x=x.
```

These are the three multiplication constraints of E255 turned around. This
is **not** E203 in general. The addition magma on `ZMod 3` satisfies E255,
but its left division `x⋆y=y-x` fails E203 at `x=1`.

Neither the left nor the right image of finite E255 models can be
characterized by a single magma identity. The Lean proof applies the existing
positive-weight affine-family obstruction: its source operations are
idempotent and hence satisfy E255, while any identity valid in all of the
division models also holds in Boolean join, which lacks both divisions.
An elementary way to see the obstruction is to use `x*y=2y-x` on odd cyclic
groups, whose left division is the averaging operation `(x+y)/2`. An identity
true in all such averaging magmas must have the same variables on both sides,
and so must hold in Boolean join. Reversing the source operation gives the
right-division obstruction.

The negative results, right-division invariance, triangle criterion, and
three-element counterexample are checked in
[`Equation255Images.lean`](../equational_theories/Parastrophes/Equation255Images.lean).
They clarify why an exact E9321/E203 reformulation can exist within E677 even
though there is no single equation for E255's entire division image.
