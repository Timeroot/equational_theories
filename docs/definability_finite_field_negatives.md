# One-way FO negatives from finite fields

20 September 2026. The eight obstructions below have passed independent
enumeration, guarded Lean builds, and the complete table audit. They are
included in the fourth checkpoint of the ongoing research pass.

| Source | Targets | Carrier | Compatible operations |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| E677 | E335, E3352, E3558, E3748, E4290, E4408 | 97 | 912,673 |
| E670 | E4290, E4408 | 49 | 2,401 |

These are **finite one-way FO-definability negatives**, the
strongest negative level in the table. Such a negative also rules out term
definability and both forms of mutual definability, on finite and arbitrary
carriers.

For the 97-element witness, let `H` be the nonzero cubes in `F_97`. Define
`x ◇ y = x + f(y − x)`, with `f(0) = 0` and `f(a d) = a f(d)` for `a ∈ H`.
The following values determine this source operation:

| Prime | Representatives `d` of the three cosets | Values `f(d)` |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 97 | 1, 2, 4 | 76, 52, 92 |

The 49-element witness uses the additive space `F_7²`, encoded by
`(a,b) ↦ a + 7b`. Its stabilizer is the group generated by the matrices
`[[0,6],[1,0]]`, `[[2,3],[3,5]]`, and `3I`. This group has order 24 and
two orbits on the nonzero vectors, represented by `(1,0)` and `(1,1)`.
The operation is again `x ◇ y = x + f(y − x)`, with `f(0) = 0`,
`f(1,0) = (3,6)`, `f(1,1) = (2,1)`, and `f(hv) = h f(v)`.

Translations and the indicated stabilizer permutations are source automorphisms.
Every operation definable without parameters in the source must preserve
them. The orbit chart describes every operation preserving those permutations:
there are exactly `97³` or `49²` possibilities, and none satisfies the indicated target
laws. This argument needs only forward definability; it does not assume that
the source can be recovered from the target.

The independent checker reconstructs the orbit chart from its generators,
checks its transports and stabilizers, verifies the source tables and laws,
and checks the entire compatible family. It evaluates large families from the
chart without allocating all their multiplication tables.

The Lean certificates use
[FiniteSymmetry.lean](../equational_theories/Definability/FiniteSymmetry.lean)
for the classification argument. The array cache in
[CachedWordImages.lean](../equational_theories/Definability/CachedWordImages.lean)
is proved equal to the original permutation-word action using ordinary Lean
proofs. The finite checks use explicit `native_decide` computation axioms;
external search output is not taken as an axiom.
