# The minimum-degree-three reduction is now complete

Updated 19 September 2026. The earlier dichotomy on this page—full
core, or a three-central configuration of order divisible by sixty—
has been superseded by the
[full-core theorem at minimum degree three](1485_min_three_full_core.md).

Every finite E1485 magma of minimum translation degree three has
order **`9·2^j`**, and every such order is realized at this minimum.
The linked theorem includes the complete argument, its five explicit
finite profile dependencies, and reproducible verification commands.
It is not yet a Lean theorem.

The general square-or-double-square spectrum conjecture remains open
at minimum degrees four and above. See the
[current proof frontier](1485_proof_status.md).
