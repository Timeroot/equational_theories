# E1485 → E1483: the twisted Boolean test model

17 September 2026. This direction remains completely open on the checked board.
The completed [prime-order obstruction](definability_weak_central_prime.md)
settles E1486 → E1485, not this reverse direction.

The cyclically twisted Boolean model on five bits has order 32 and operation
`x*y = 31 XOR (rotateLeft(x) AND rotateRight(y))`. It satisfies E1485 and
fails E1483. Its idempotents are `11, 13, 21, 22, 26`, permuted cyclically by
rotation. Every term operation fixes these idempotents and commutes with the
rotation. Consequently the untwisted Boolean NAND operation, which has no
idempotents, cannot itself be a term operation of this model.

[definability_weak_central_check.py](../scripts/definability_weak_central_check.py)
reproduces the following finite computations:

- The subalgebras generated by at most two elements consist of five singletons,
  one two-element subalgebra, five four-element subalgebras, and the full carrier.
- Each two- or four-element subalgebra has exactly 16 binary term operations,
  of which two satisfy E1483. On every four-element subalgebra these are exactly
  its original operation and its opposite. Rotation carries these five
  subalgebras around one orbit, so a term realizing E1483 must make the same
  original/opposite choice on all five.
- Exhaustive enumeration through eight leaves gives **78,546 distinct binary
  term operations**, none satisfying E1483. The numbers first appearing at
  sizes 1–8 are `2, 4, 16, 80, 392, 2160, 11680, 64212`.

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2 python3 scripts/definability_weak_central_check.py
```

Repeated operation tables are represented by a shortest term; substituting a
shorter equivalent subterm preserves the resulting table, so this deduplication
does not lose operations within the bound. A sampled triple can reject a table
only by exhibiting a failure of E1483; every surviving table is checked on all
32³ triples. These are Python computations, not Lean certificates, and they
do not exclude longer terms or change any board status.

Two exploratory Z3 searches also returned `unknown`: the full 32-element E1483
constraints with the prescribed rotation and idempotents reached a 60-second
solver limit; a search additionally restricting the small subalgebras to their
possible target term operations reached a 20-second limit after adding 1,843
law instances. These are unresolved searches, not refutations or target models.
The next useful step is an algebraic obstruction from these local restrictions,
or a complete target operation satisfying them.

The [subsequent rank and constant-row theorems](definability_1483_rank_constraints.md)
prove that a mutually term-recoverable E1483 companion here could have neither
a constant nor a bijective row: it must have exactly five idempotents, whereas
a nontrivial finite E1483 magma with a constant row has its idempotents in
orbits of size three. These constraints and verified symmetries are now
available in the maintained recovery search. This direction remains open.
