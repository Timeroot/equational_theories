# E667: permutation parity, five-point designs, and small-fiber extensions

Research date: 2026-10-04. Write the law as

\[
 y*(x*((x*x)*y))=x,
\]

and put \(D(x)=x*x\), \(L_x(y)=x*y\). Every finite E667 magma is a
quasigroup. The new general parity and three-fiber obstructions below are
proved in Lean. The five-design characterization, binary-extension theory,
and affine polynomial conditions and decomposition have also now been
formalized; see the [proof index](e667_formalization_20261004.md).
They are structural restrictions, not new exclusions for unrestricted E667.

The spectrum still has these **17 open orders**:

```
12, 15, 24, 30, 39, 48, 51, 60, 75, 87, 96, 102, 159, 174, 195, 219, 339.
```

The follow-up [nonlinear construction note](e667_nonlinear_constructions_20261004.md)
gives explicit binary formulas, classifies the three isomorphism types of
two-fiber extensions of the five-point mean algebra, and develops constructions
from nonabelian groups and involutive automorphisms.

## 1. No commutative models of order three modulo four

**Proved in Lean**, without a finite-search certificate:
`Spectrum.E667.not_commutative_of_card_mod_four_three` in
`Spectrum/Equation667CommutativeParity.lean`.

For each output symbol \(x\), let \(M_x(y)\) be the unique solution \(z\)
of \(y*z=x\). E667 says

\[
 M_x=L_x L_{D(x)}.
\]

Commutativity says that \(M_x\) is an involution. Its fixed points are exactly
those \(y\) for which \(D(y)=x\). If the order \(n\) is odd, every involution
has a fixed point, so every \(x\) has a square root. Finiteness makes \(D\)
bijective. Consequently every \(M_x\) has exactly one fixed point and
\((n-1)/2\) transpositions.

If \(n\equiv3\pmod4\), every \(M_x\) has sign \(-1\). There are an odd
number of them, so their signs have product \(-1\). On the other hand,

\[
 \prod_x\operatorname{sgn}(M_x)
 =\prod_x\operatorname{sgn}(L_x)\operatorname{sgn}(L_{D(x)})
 =\left(\prod_x\operatorname{sgn}(L_x)\right)^2=1,
\]

because \(D\) is a permutation. Contradiction.

This excludes commutative models at **every remaining open odd order**:
15, 39, 51, 75, 87, 159, 195, 219, and 339. It needs neither simplicity nor
an idempotent. The old public order-15 theorem now applies this general
result. Its 8,607,357-byte compressed SAT certificate, special encoding, and
certificate-generation script have been retired from the public source/data
paths. `667_commutative_fifteen.json` records the replacement and old hash.

## 2. Commutative idempotent models are exactly five-point design algebras

**Proved in Lean** in `Equation667CommutativeBlocks.lean` and
`Equation667CommutativeDesign.lean`: the five-point embedding, unique block
cover, mean-algebra coordinates on each block, both counting formulas, and
`card_mod_twenty`. The gluing converse is in `Equation667BinaryDesign.lean`;
`Equation667BinaryDesignExistence.lean` recovers the original multiplication
from the extracted blocks.

Suppose the entire magma is commutative and idempotent. The law reduces to E63, \(y*(x*(x*y))=x\). Section 1 now gives
\(M_a=L_a^2\), an involution whose unique fixed point is \(a\). Thus every
cycle of \(L_a\) other than its fixed point \(a\) has length four.

Choose \(a\ne b\) and set
\(c=a*b\), \(d=a*c\), \(e=a*d\). These five points are distinct. The full
multiplication table is forced:

| * | a | b | c | d | e |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | a | c | d | e | b |
| b | c | b | e | a | d |
| c | d | e | c | b | a |
| d | e | a | b | d | c |
| e | b | d | a | c | e |

Here is a derivation of all the off-diagonal entries beyond the defining
row. The four-cycle gives \(a*e=b\). E63 at \((a,b)\) and \((a,c)\) gives
\(b*d=a\) and \(c*e=a\). E63 at \((c,a)\) gives
\(a*(c*d)=c=a*b\), hence \(c*d=b\). Similarly its instances \((d,a)\)
and \((e,a)\) give \(d*e=c\) and \(e*b=d\). Finally E63 at \((b,c)\)
gives \(c*(b*(b*c))=b=c*d\); cancel twice, using \(b*e=d\), to obtain
\(b*c=e\). Commutativity and idempotence fill the rest.

This is the five-point affine algebra \(x*y=3(x+y)\) over \(\mathbb F_5\),
with labels \((a,b,c,d,e)=(0,1,3,4,2)\). Every pair in it generates all five
points. Consequently the five-element submagmas form a **2-(n,5,1) design**:
each pair belongs to exactly one block, since its generated submagma is
already the whole block. Conversely, give every block of any 2-(n,5,1)
design a copy of this five-point operation. Use its multiplication on the
unique block containing a distinct pair, and put \(x*x=x\). Every E667
instance stays inside its pair's block, so this is a commutative idempotent
E667 model. The construction and extraction are inverse once the block
operations are retained as part of the design data.

In particular, for \(n>1\), a point belongs to \((n-1)/4\) blocks and the
total number of blocks is \(n(n-1)/20\). Thus

\[
 n\equiv1\text{ or }5\pmod{20}.
\]

The singleton also satisfies this congruence. Sufficiency at a specific
admissible order is precisely the corresponding design-existence question;
this note does not formalize or import the general design-existence theorem.
The classical literature includes H. Hanani, *The Existence and Construction
of Balanced Incomplete Block Designs*, Ann. Math. Stat. 32 (1961), 361–386,
DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177705047.

For a commutative E667 magma, squaring is an endomorphism (already proved by
`square_hom_of_commutative`), so its idempotents are closed under the
operation. If nonempty, that submagma therefore also has size 1 or 5 modulo
20. **Neither closure nor this argument extends to general E667 magmas.**
The saved ten-point counterexample even has commuting idempotent pairs
whose product is not idempotent. The assertion above assumes global
commutativity; a single commuting pair is insufficient for that example.

## 3. A quotient with a short square cycle cannot have three-point fibers

**Proved in Lean** in `Spectrum/Equation667FiberThreePeriodTwo.lean`:
`no_fiber_extension` and the coordinate-free `no_quotient`.

Let \(q\) be an E667 quotient. If some point \(a\) satisfies
\(D^2(a)=a\), then no finite E667 magma maps onto it with all fibers of size
three. This includes an idempotent or a genuine two-cycle of the square map.
The eight-point obstruction now follows from this general theorem; its sign
argument no longer depends on inspecting the eight-point multiplication
table.

Label each fiber by \(\mathbb F_3\). Cancellation makes every block a Latin
operation. Every Latin operation on three points has the affine form

\[
 (i,x)*(j,y)=(q(i,j), A_{ij}x+B_{ij}y+C_{ij}),\qquad A_{ij},B_{ij}\in\{1,-1\}.
\]

For example, a permutation of three points is affine. Applying this in each
variable gives a bilinear formula; its mixed coefficient must vanish because
otherwise some row would have zero slope. The labels, constants, and slopes
can vary independently between blocks: this is not an assumption of a
uniform affine extension.

Put \(s_i=q(i,i)\), \(t_{ij}=q(s_i,j)\), and \(u_{ij}=q(i,t_{ij})\).
Comparing the two coefficients in E667 gives

\[
 B_{j,u}(A_{i,t}+B_{i,t}A_{s_i,j}(A_{ii}+B_{ii}))=1,
 \qquad
 A_{j,u}+B_{j,u}B_{i,t}B_{s_i,j}=0.
\]

Since every nonzero scalar squares to one, these imply

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{ij}:&\quad A_{i,t}B_{j,u}A_{ii}B_{ii}=-1,\\
 Q_{ij}:&\quad A_{j,u}B_{j,u}B_{i,t}B_{s_i,j}=-1.
 \end{aligned}
\]

If \(A_{ii}B_{ii}=1\), the first coefficient also gives
\(T_{ij}: A_{ii}B_{i,t}A_{s_i,j}B_{j,u}=1\). These elementary sign
identities are checked over \(\mathbb F_3\) by the ordinary Lean kernel.

Now set \(b=q(a,a)\), \(c=q(a,b)\), \(d=q(b,c)\). The hypothesis says
\(q(b,b)=a\), and E667 at \((b,b)\) says \(q(b,d)=b\). Therefore

| (i,j) | t(i,j) | u(i,j) |
|---|---|---|
| (a,b) | a | b |
| (b,a) | b | a |
| (a,d) | b | c |
| (b,b) | c | d |

Multiplying \(P_{ab}Q_{ba}\) gives \(A_{aa}B_{aa}=1\); multiplying
\(P_{ab}Q_{ab}\) gives \(A_{bb}B_{bb}=1\). We can now multiply

\[
 Q_{ab}\,P_{ad}\,Q_{bb}\,T_{ad}\,T_{bb}.
\]

Every sign on the left occurs an even number of times. The product on the
right is \((-1)^3=-1\): contradiction. The argument does not require
\(a,b,c,d\) to be distinct.

This gives a new condition on any prospective three-fiber quotient: **its
square map can have no cycle of length one or two**. It does not yet rule
out longer square cycles. In particular the idempotent-free five-point
quotient has a five-cycle and still uses its separate, already formalized
obstruction. No blanket claim that all three-fiber extensions are impossible
is established here.

## 4. Complete affine equations, and why 2 and 5 are special

**Proved in Lean** in `Equation667AffineStructure.lean` and
`Equation667AffineDecomposition.lean`. The endomorphism-coefficient statements
do not require finiteness or invertibility of the coefficients; the
idempotent and splitting results use the cardinality coprimality condition.

On a finite abelian group consider
\(x*y=Ax+By+c\), initially with arbitrary automorphisms \(A,B\).
Comparing coefficients in E667 forces \(A=-B^3\); in particular the
operators commute. The remaining conditions are exactly

\[
 P(B)=0,\qquad K(B)c=0,
\]

where

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P(t)&=t^8-t^6-t^4-1\\
     &=(t^2+1)(t^3-t-1)(t^3-t+1),\\
 K(t)&=-t^5+t^2+t+1=-(t^2+1)(t^3-t-1).
 \end{aligned}
\]

These conditions are also sufficient by substitution. Write the three
factors as \(F,G,H\) in that order. Since \(D(x)=(B-B^3)x+c\), an
idempotent is exactly a solution to \(H(B)x=c\). The integral identity

\[
 (-2t^4-t^3+2t^2+3t+6)H(t)+(-2t^2-t+4)K(t)=10
\]

has an immediate consequence: **every affine E667 magma whose order is
coprime to 10 has an idempotent**. Indeed multiplication by 10 is invertible,
so apply the identity to \(c\), use \(K(B)c=0\), and divide by 10. Translating
this idempotent to zero removes the constant term.

The pairwise resultants have absolute values 5, 5, and 8. Away from
characteristics 2 and 5, the three factors are pairwise comaximal, and the
abelian group splits into the three corresponding \(B\)-invariant summands:

* On the \(F\)-summand, \(B^2=-1\) and \(A=B\): the Gaussian,
  commutative family.
* On the \(H\)-summand, \(B^3=B-1\), so \(A=1-B\): the idempotent affine
  E63 family.
* On the \(G\)-summand, \(B^3=B+1\), so \(A=-B-1\): the negative of an
  idempotent affine E63 operation (use right coefficient \(-B\)).

This decomposition applies to all finite abelian groups of order coprime
to 10, not just vector spaces. The resultants make the polynomial factors
comaximal after inverting 10, so the module Chinese remainder theorem
applies. The Lean proof avoids an abstract CRT invocation: it writes three
projection numerators whose sum is 10, proves that each annihilates the
other two factor kernels, and divides by ten. `unique_splitting` gives the
unique three-part decomposition of each element. The denominators therefore
need only divide ten. At primes 2 and 5 the factors can overlap. This explains why the
idempotent-free affine examples at orders 5 and 8 escape the argument.
It is not an assertion that arbitrary nonlinear E667 magmas of order
coprime to 10 have idempotents.

There is also a uniform affine exclusion, now proved in
`Equation667AffineThree.lean`: **if three divides the carrier order, then
nine divides it**. A nonzero element a of order three cannot have
`B(a)=0,a,-a`, by the polynomial P. Hence a and B(a) generate a subgroup of
order nine. All 17 currently open E667 orders are 3 or 6 modulo nine, so
none can have an affine model on any finite abelian group.

The [formalization report](e667_formalization_20261004.md) also gives a complete
paper characterization of the affine spectrum. A positive n is possible
exactly when every prime dividing it to exponent one admits a root of P.
The converse uses universal integral matrices in dimensions two and three,
then direct products: every exponent at least two is a sum of twos and threes.
Those matrix constructions and the prime obstruction are formalized; the
final prime-factorization assembly is not yet a single Lean theorem.

## 5. Two-point fibers lead to a linear cocycle problem

**General theory proved in Lean** in `Equation667BinaryExtensions.lean`:
the cocycle criterion, completeness for arbitrary two-element fiber blocks,
and gauge relabelling. The numerical ranks at the end of this section remain
external exploratory calculations, except for the five-point mean quotient,
whose full classification is now formalized.

Every Latin operation on two points has the form \(x+y+C\). Consequently
all two-fiber extensions of a quotient \(q\), after labelling the fibers,
are exactly

\[
 (i,x)*(j,y)=(q(i,j),x+y+C_{ij}),\qquad x,y\in\mathbb F_2.
\]

Substitution into E667 gives the homogeneous linear system

\[
 C_{ii}+C_{s_i,j}+C_{i,t_{ij}}+C_{j,u_{ij}}=0.
\]

Relabelling a fiber by \(x\mapsto x+h_i\) changes the cocycle by
\(C_{ij}\mapsto C_{ij}+h_i+h_j+h_{q(i,j)}\). Thus these extensions can be
searched by linear algebra, modulo this relabelling, instead of a table SAT
search. Commutativity adds the linear conditions \(C_{ij}=C_{ji}\), when
the quotient is commutative.

For the saved order-2 class, both order-4 classes, and the five order-5
classes, the cocycle-space dimensions are respectively

```
2; 5, 5; 5, 5, 9, 9, 5.
```

For the three commutative order-5 classes, imposing commutativity gives
dimension 5 in each case, equal to the relabelling-space dimension. Hence
every commutative double cover of any of those three quotients is the direct
product with the two-element group. These finite calculations are
reproducible with `scripts/spectrum_667_structure.py`; they are not Lean
certificates. No new spectrum seed results from these double covers.

## 6. Open structural questions and bounded negative results

The square map is not generally an endomorphism, and the idempotents need
not be closed. The saved nine- and ten-point examples prevent using those
shortcuts. It remains worth investigating whether the *kernel relation*
\(D(x)=D(y)\), or a relation defined by eventual square iterates, must be a
congruence in useful subclasses. That would turn simplicity into strong
restrictions on the diagonal.

The bounded ATP probes in this pass did **not** settle the kernel-congruence
claims. Four Prover9 division-symbol encodings used maximum weight 28 and
120-second limits; they saturated without a proof, with weight pruning, so
this is not a completeness result. Three right-identity probes with weight
70 and 120-second limits timed out. Four probes of the consequences of a
commuting pair in a globally idempotent magma used 60-second limits and also
found no proof.

Mace4 searches for a diagonal that is both noninjective and nonconstant
exhausted order 7, but timed out at orders 9, 10, and 11 with 90-second CPU
limits. In fact a saved order-10 example has just such a diagonal. These
runs must not be described as excluding nonuniform diagonals or as evidence
of kernel congruence. Exact inputs, statuses, and input hashes are retained
in `data/spectrum/667_structure_research.json`.

The strongest next unresolved direction is understanding noncommutative,
simple E667 quasigroups with nonconstant diagonal. The new results narrow
that direction, but do not close orders 12 or 15 or reduce the 17-order list.
