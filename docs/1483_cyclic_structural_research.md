# The cyclic Boolean cube separates E1483 and E1485 structurally

28 September 2026. An eight-element E1483 magma supplies a finite
FO-structural obstruction to E1485. The final finite obstruction is checked
separately in Lean as
`Definability.Cyclic1483.Equation1485_not_structuralFromFin_Equation1483`
in [`Cyclic1483Structural.lean`](../equational_theories/Definability/Cyclic1483Structural.lean).
Its finite helper is `Definability.Cyclic1483.enlarged_symmetry`; see the
[certificate note](1483_cyclic8_certificate.md) for the guarded LRAT-checker
axiom and reproduction details. This note gives an independent description of the source
and a conceptual reduction of the target problem to constant-row magmas.
It distinguishes that completed reduction from a proposed Boolean
classification whose missing premise has not been supplied.

## The source and its automorphisms

Identify the carrier with the Boolean cube B={0,1}³, encoded as integers 0–7.
Let r rotate the three coordinates:

    r = (1 2 4)(3 6 5),
    f(x,y) = NOT(r(x) AND r²(y)).

Here NOT and AND are coordinatewise Boolean operations. The points 0 and 7
are the Boolean bottom and top. The operation is a cubic twist of ordinary
Boolean NAND, and satisfies E1483 by the existing cubic-twist identity.
It has the constant row f(0,x)=7.

Its automorphism group is exactly the cyclic group generated by r. There
is a short conceptual argument avoiding a scan through 8! permutations:

1. The constant row is unique, so an automorphism fixes 0 and 7.
2. The operation intrinsically defines
   `phi(x)=f(7,f(7,x))=r(x)`. Every automorphism therefore commutes with r.
3. The intrinsic untwist `f(r²(x),r(y))` is ordinary Boolean NAND.
   Every source automorphism is consequently an automorphism of the
   three-atom Boolean algebra, whose automorphism group is S3.
4. Conversely, every Boolean automorphism commuting with r preserves f.
   Thus `Aut(f)=C_S3(r)=C3`.

The source has an E1485 operation first-order definable from it: ordinary
NAND is already given by the displayed intrinsic untwist. Therefore this
source does **not** refute one-way finite FO-definability of E1485 from
E1483. The obstruction concerns recovery of the source from the target.

## The finite group criterion

A parameter-free definable operation inherits every automorphism of its
source. Consequently, if an E1485 operation q were mutually first-order
definable with f, then

    Aut(q) = Aut(f) = <r>.

The checked finite certificate proves the following narrower statement,
which is enough without classifying Aut(q): every E1485 operation q on
these eight points commuting with r also commutes with at least one of

    p0 = (2 4)(3 6),
    p1 = (2 4)(3 5),
    p2 = (2 4)(5 6).

Each permutation fixes 0 and 7. None preserves f. Indeed, each reverses r
by conjugation, whereas every automorphism of f commutes with r. If f
were definable from q, any one of these extra q-automorphisms would have
to preserve f, a contradiction.

This refutes the finite FO-structural direction E1483 to E1485, and hence
also that direction over arbitrary carriers. It separates their structural
equivalence classes. It does not establish any spectrum nonexistence.

## A completed conceptual reduction: the target has a constant row

Suppose q satisfies E1485 on eight points and r is an automorphism of q.
The following argument needs no classification of all eight-point tables.
Write `L_a(x)=q(a,x)` and let d(a) be its image size.

Three general finite E1485 facts have already been proved in Lean in
`Spectrum/WeakCentral/Finite.lean` and `Spectrum/WeakCentralCardinality.lean`:

* Every nonempty fiber of L_a has the same size t, with d(a)t=8.
* If b lies in the image of L_a, then 8≤d(a)d(b).
* A bijective row supplies a constant column, and a constant column
  supplies a constant row.

The fixed points of r are exactly 0 and 7. They form a two-element E1485
submagma, since q preserves fixed points. The two-element E1485 operation
is Boolean NAND up to relabeling; this is an elementary check of the four
entries. Relabel these two fixed points as z and u so that

    q(z,z)=q(z,u)=q(u,z)=u,   q(u,u)=z.

This relabeling does not change the two three-cycles of r. In particular,
L_u maps the two fixed points bijectively onto themselves.

Consider either fiber of L_u over z or u. It is invariant under r and
contains exactly one fixed point of r. Its other points come in orbits of
three. Hence its size t is 1 modulo 3. Uniform fibers also give t|8, so

    t=1 or t=4.

If t=4 then d(u)=2. But q(u,z)=u means that u lies in the image of L_u.
The second general E1485 fact would then give 8≤d(u)²=4, impossible.
Therefore t=1, so L_u is bijective. The third general fact supplies a
constant row of q.

The constant row is unique, so its index is fixed by r. Thus it is one of
the two rows indexed 0 and 7. This establishes a concrete additional
restriction that could shorten a future finite proof of the extra-
automorphism assertion.

This conceptual constant-row reduction is a complete paper argument.
It has not been installed as a separate Lean theorem in this pass; the
finite certificate proves the needed extra-automorphism assertion directly.

## A tempting classification shortcut, and its missing premise

All the target tables found during the search were relabeled copies of
ordinary Boolean NAND. If every constant-row E1485 magma were known to
be a Boolean NAND algebra, the remainder would be conceptual as well:
an eight-element Boolean algebra has three atoms and automorphism group
S3. Its atoms and coatoms form the two three-cycles of r. Choosing the
atom/coatoms permutation whose restriction to {1,2,4} swaps 2 and 4 gives
one of p0,p1,p2 on the full carrier.

However, the available constant-row untwisting theorem assumes **E1483**.
It cannot be applied to a target assumed only to satisfy E1485. Neither
general implication E1485 to E1483 nor its constant-row special case is
being assumed here. Thus the argument above is not a proof of this
Boolean classification.

For clarity, a conditional calculation explains why the shortcut initially
looked promising. If a constant-row operation f additionally satisfies
E1483, its existing untwist B is commutative E1485. Commutative E1485
implies the Sheffer single axiom E345169: commute the innermost factors
and use `CentralDual.left_regular`. The existing Sheffer development then
identifies B with Boolean NAND. The original operation has the form

    f(x,y)=NOT(phi(x) AND phi²(y)),   phi³=id.

If this f also satisfies E1485, expansion in the Boolean algebra gives

    x = x AND (phi²(y) OR NOT(phi²(z) AND y)).

Putting x=z=top yields y≤phi²(y). Applying phi cyclically forces equality,
so phi=id and f itself is ordinary NAND. This correctly proves the
classification **under both E1483 and E1485**, but does not supply the
missing E1483 hypothesis for arbitrary constant-row E1485 targets.

Bounded Vampire queries for commutativity, involutivity of `q(u,·)`, and
E1483 from constant-row E1485 were exploratory. A timeout is not evidence
for or against any of these assertions. None is a dependency of the finite
Lean obstruction.
