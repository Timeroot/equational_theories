# E667: further restrictions and construction probes

The new structural results below have complete Lean proofs. The unrestricted
spectrum remains unchanged: the 17 unresolved orders are

```
12, 15, 24, 30, 39, 48, 51, 60, 75, 87, 96, 102, 159, 174, 195, 219, 339.
```

The existing fully formalized tail is still every order at least 340. None of
the construction timeouts described below is a nonexistence result for magmas.

## No commutative model at any order 3 times a power of two

`Spectrum.E667.not_commutative_three_mul_power_two` in
`Spectrum/Equation667CommutativePowers.lean` rules out commutative models at
every order `3 * 2^k`. This extends the earlier order-twelve exclusion to
24, 48, 96, and the entire infinite family, without adding a search certificate.

The proof establishes a slightly stronger induction statement: a commutative
E667 model whose order divides `3 * 2^k` has an idempotent and has order prime
to three. Squaring is an endomorphism. If it is injective, the involution
parity argument makes the order odd, hence either one or three; three is
already impossible. Otherwise its image is a smaller model, so induction
supplies an idempotent `e` and an image order prime to three. The fiber over
`e` is closed and has constant square `e`, which makes its order prime to
three too. Uniform fiber sizes show that the original order is the product
of these two orders. The idempotent in the image is also an idempotent in
the original magma, completing the induction.

## The idempotent-free eight-point model is unique

`Spectrum.E667.EightClassification.exists_iso` classifies every
idempotent-free E667 operation on eight points. It is the affine operation

```
x * y = (1+t)x + ty + 1,    over F₂[t]/(t³+t+1).
```

`Equation667EightBase.lean` records its table after relabelling the first row
as `(0 1 2 3 4 5 6)(7)`. Normalization does not assume this cycle type: in
any Latin square, some row has a fixed point. Move that row's index to zero
and apply the already proved chain-labelling theorem. The certificate then
checks that a chain-labelled, idempotent-free table with a fixed point in
row zero must be the displayed table.

The CNF has 1,024 variables and 23,815 clauses. Its trimmed LRAT certificate
is **155,951 bytes compressed**; Lean's existing verified LRAT checker accepts
it. No order-24 table enumeration is involved. The classification also gives
the Lean theorems `EightClassification.square_involutive` and
`EightClassification.medial`.

## No three-element-fiber extension of that model

`Spectrum.E667.FiberEight.no_fiber_extension` is an algebraic obstruction,
with no SAT certificate. Label the three points of each fiber by `F₃`.
Every Latin three-point block is affine, so any extension must have the form

```
(i,a) * (j,b) = (q(i,j), Aᵢⱼ a + Bᵢⱼ b + Cᵢⱼ),
```

where `Aᵢⱼ,Bᵢⱼ` are signs and the constants are arbitrary. Substituting into
E667 and comparing coefficients gives, with `s=q(i,i)`, `t=q(s,j)`,
`u=q(i,t)`,

```
Aᵢₜ Bⱼᵤ Aᵢᵢ Bᵢᵢ = -1,
Aⱼᵤ Bⱼᵤ Bᵢₜ Bₛⱼ = -1.
```

When `Aᵢᵢ Bᵢᵢ=1`, the first coefficient additionally gives
`Aᵢᵢ Bᵢₜ Aₛⱼ Bⱼᵤ=1`. Only a few instances are needed. The cases `(0,1)`
and `(1,0)` force both diagonal products at 0 and 1 to equal one. The
first identity at `(0,4)`, the second at `(1,1)`, the two corresponding
additional identities, and the second identity at `(0,1)` multiply to
`1=-1`. All constants have already canceled. This excludes arbitrary Latin
three-point blocks, including blocks with different coefficients and origins.

## Consequences for order twenty-four

`Equation667TwentyFourRestrictions.lean` proves:

- No order-24 model has an eight-element quotient. An idempotent in that
  quotient would give a forbidden three-element submagma. Otherwise use the
  eight-point classification and the preceding extension obstruction.
- The only possible quotient orders are **1, 2, 12, and 24**.
- A proper nontrivial quotient therefore gives an order-12 model: either the
  quotient itself has order 12, or the fiber over an idempotent in a
  two-element quotient does. Thus, if order 12 is eventually excluded, every
  possible order-24 model must be simple.

These statements do not exclude unrestricted order-24 models. Commutativity
at 24 is excluded separately by the general descent proof above.

## A useful counterexample to a stronger squaring claim

The ten-element model in `Equation667RetractCounterexample.lean` has
`D²=D`, but `D` is not a homomorphism and its idempotents are not closed.
All three facts are checked in Lean. This strengthens the earlier warning
that closure of idempotents is not a valid general search constraint.

Its five square fibers each have size two. They **do** form a congruence,
despite the failure of the square map to preserve multiplication. In fiber
coordinates the operation has the form
`(i,a)*(j,b)=(q(i,j),a+b+Cᵢⱼ)` over `F₂`, with an idempotent five-element
quotient. The constants prevent the zero section from being closed. This
does not settle the separate conjecture that square fibers form a congruence
when `D²=D`; bounded searches for counterexamples to that conjecture at
10 and 12 remained inconclusive.

The [later nonlinear construction note](e667_nonlinear_constructions_20261004.md)
now recovers this ten-point table from the explicit formula
`(i,a)*(j,b)=(3(i+j),a+b+v(i)+v(2j-i))` with `v=[0,1,1,0,0]`.
The same note classifies its binary extension family and explains how to
make independent modifications on larger five-point designs.

## Construction searches

The accompanying `data/spectrum/667_followup_20261002.json` records exact
inputs, limits, outcomes, and retained near-designs.

- Three-block replacement was exhausted for the saved near cyclic design
  at 159. Every successful repair would be a full difference-family
  certificate, but none exists with two of those five blocks frozen.
- New cyclic searches at 195 used block sizes `{7,7,11}`, and a relative
  difference family with one 11-block, four 5-blocks, and a subgroup of order
  five. The latter's cosets provide the remaining 5-blocks. Four ten-minute
  runs found no design; the relative searches reached two collisions. Exact
  three-block replacement of both retained relative near-designs also failed.
- A second construction approach glues models along a common submagma,
  requiring every point outside that submagma to be idempotent. Together
  with transversal designs, a nine-point filler with a two-point hole would
  give order 51, while an eleven-point filler with a four-point hole would
  give order 39. The tested small fillers and the precise scope of each
  negative or timeout are recorded in the data file. These are restrictions
  on this construction, not exclusions at 39 or 51.
  Mace4 exhausted the nine-point case and both four-point hole types in the
  eleven-point case. The larger probes at 17 and 19 timed out.
- Additional design probes use the noncommutative groups
  `(C₁₃ ⋊ C₃) × C₅` and `C₇₃ ⋊ C₃`, replacing cyclic distances by inverse
  pairs of group differences. Their recorded outcomes concern only the
  bounded searches, not all such designs.
  Four three-minute runs found no design, reaching two collisions at 219
  and three at 195.

The general right-identity questions remain open: whether its left
translation has sixth power one, whether it is an automorphism, and whether
the square fiber over it is closed. The attempted Prover9 searches supplied
no proof. Small exhaustive countermodel searches at orders 8 and 9 do not
establish these assertions generally.


The classification script's `--solve` option regenerates and trims its
certificate. The new public conclusions have `spectrum_assert ... complete`
audits. The extension obstruction has only the standard Lean axioms; the
classification uses the existing native LRAT-checking mechanism, and the
commutative descent uses the previously verified native exclusion at order
three. None of the new conclusions depends on `sorry`.
